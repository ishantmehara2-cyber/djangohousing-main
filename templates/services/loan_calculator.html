Transparent Mathematical Model

This calculator uses standard present-value annuity calculation models. Banks cap total monthly EMI commitments (existing EMIs + proposed home loan EMI) up to FOIR (50%) of your net monthly income.

Formula used: Present Value $PV = EMI \times \frac{(1+r)^n - 1}{r(1+r)^n}$, where $r$ is the monthly interest rate and $n$ is total tenure in months.

*Disclaimer: Results shown are estimates for guidance only. Actual home loan approval depends on credit score, property appraisal, age, legal verification, and bank policies.
