Home Loan Eligibility Criteria

  • ▪
    Age: Minimum 21 years at loan application up to 65 years at loan maturity.
  • ▪
    Employment: Minimum 2 years work experience for salaried individuals or 3 years business vintage for self-employed professionals.
  • ▪
    Credit Score: CIBIL score of 750 or above unlocks lowest interest rates and faster approval.
  • ▪
    FOIR Norms: Monthly EMI obligations typically capped between 40% to 60% of net monthly income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum home loan amount I can get?

Banks generally fund up to 80%-90% of the property's agreement value depending on your income, age, credit score, and existing monthly EMI obligations.

Are interest rates fixed or floating?

Most home loans in India are offered on floating interest rates tied to RBI's Repo Rate (RLLR). Fixed interest rate options are also available with select lenders.

Does Housing charge any fee for loan assistance?

No. Our home loan advisory and rate comparison services are completely FREE for property buyers.
